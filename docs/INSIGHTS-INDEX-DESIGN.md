# Insights index design

The Insights index is intentionally designed as a restrained clinical journal rather than a service landing page or high-volume blog.

## Design principles

- Readers should reach article choices immediately after a short editorial introduction.
- Premium positioning comes from typography, negative space, proportion and restraint rather than decorative graphics.
- The index uses two large editorial columns on tablet/desktop instead of a dense three-column blog grid.
- Topic filters are compact and horizontally scrollable on mobile.
- Article summaries remain short and reading-time metadata stays visible.
- The former neural motif, oversized featured-article treatment, Veterans duplicate section and index-level Medical Authorship block are intentionally removed.
- Authorship belongs on the article itself, where it supports clinical credibility without delaying article discovery.
- The bottom contact invitation remains, but is visually quieter than the editorial content.

## CMS copy

`Insights Settings` continues to control the intro heading/body and bottom contact CTA. Keep the intro body to one short sentence. The `medicalAuthorship` setting remains in the CMS for backward compatibility but is no longer rendered on the index.
